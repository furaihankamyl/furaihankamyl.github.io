## The Children Behind the Walls

**PKM-RSH Research Funding Grantee**
**"Network Governance in Supporting the Subjective Well-being of Juvenile Inmates in LPKA"**

LPKA is Indonesia's Lembaga Pembinaan Khusus Anak, the special correctional institution for children. The question we started with was whether the collaborations run by LPKA Kelas II Jakarta were actually organized around what the children inside needed.

We used social network analysis and a single case study design, drawing on institutional site analysis, interviews, and observation. The finding was structural rather than moral. The institution was trying, but it lacked comprehensive standard operating procedures for the programs it ran, which meant its partnerships were not consistently directed toward outcomes.

Our recommendation to the Ministry of Law and Human Rights followed from that. Build SOPs for each program, project, and policy, and let those objectives determine which partners are engaged.

This project has its own article, because the field research part of it is not something I can compress into three paragraphs.

## When Young People Do Not Trust the Reporting Channel

**PKM-AI Research Incentive Awardee**
**"Comprehensive Analysis of Public Trust Levels Among Young Individuals in the JakLapor Feature"**

JakLapor is a public reporting feature run by the DKI Jakarta Provincial Government. What we kept hearing from young users was a concern about data breach, which is a difficult thing to act on when it stays anecdotal. So we measured it.

We ran comparative tests using Mann-Whitney U and Kruskal-Wallis, and an associative study using Spearman correlation, across a questionnaire segmented by gender, region, generation, and education. The result was a significant difference in public trust between Generation Y and Generation Z, with Generation Z holding lower trust in the feature.

That is a specific problem with a specific owner. Our recommendation was that the provincial government run a public needs survey for JakLapor aimed directly at Generation Z, rather than treating young users as one undifferentiated group.

## The Cost of a Signature

**1st Place for Paper and 1st Place for Presentation, Temu Administrator Muda Indonesia 2024**
**"Simplification of Limited Liability Company Registration Procedures Through Notaries"**

![Award ceremony at Temu AdMI 2024](images/activity-indonesia-emas-2.png)

Founders in Indonesia carry procedural, time, and cost burdens to register a company that founders in other countries do not. We wanted to know how much of that burden was attributable to the notarization requirement itself.

Using independent t-test analysis across multiple case studies, we compared countries that require a notarization process against countries that do not. Administrative burden was significantly lower where notarization was not required.

We recommended to the Ministry of Investment/BKPM that the role of notaries in company registration be integrated into the process or removed from it. This was the project where the trade-off was hardest to write, because the recommendation is straightforwardly bad news for one profession.
