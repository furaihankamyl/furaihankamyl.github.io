PKBM Hasanah runs education and training for the children at LPKA Kelas II Jakarta. In our interview, they described a problem. LPKA struggled to pass on programs that had run well to new partners, so the quality of coaching differed from one partner to the next. Ruang Damai described a similar picture, with gaps in facilities and in coordination between actors at different levels.

Those two interviews framed this study. It is the PKM-RSH project I wrote about in [the PIMNAS story](article.html?slug=pimnas), done with Adinda Puspita Ningrum and Muhammad Rafli Gebrena under the supervision of Dr. Lina Miftahul Jannah. Adinda was the first author. The question was practical. How should LPKA manage its network of partners so that coaching programs survive changes in people, partners, and location?

PUSKAPA UI (2023) had already named three problems in LPKA coaching programs. They were not based on comprehensive needs assessments, resources were limited, and coordination between actors at different levels had gaps. Muzakki and Subroto (2023) added the lack of standard operating procedures.

## Mapping who works with LPKA

We started from what LPKA itself publishes. We reviewed 260 articles on the websites of LPKA Kelas II Jakarta and the Directorate General of Corrections and kept 56 that described actors in coaching programs. From those we coded 80 actors by level, time, and type of support, and mapped their interactions in Gephi 0.10.1. Then we tested the map against reality through observation at LPKA on 4 July 2024 and interviews with LPKA and its partners, from Ditjenpas and the police to Bapas Jakarta Selatan, Disdukcapil Jakarta, Ruang Damai, and PKBM Hasanah.

## What the network showed

**The network is strongest at the regional level and weakest at the local level.** Regional actors averaged 6 connections each, with a density of 0.400. Local, community, and private actors averaged 3.4, with a density of 0.065.

Level matters because it shaped what survived the move. National and regional actors stay connected to each other, so information keeps flowing even when one organization drops out. Local actors tend to stand alone. When LPKA moved from Salemba to Cinere in 2020, local partners around Cempaka Putih were replaced by local organizations around Cinere. Some programs that had worked well could not be run again.

**The network also became looser after the move.** Density fell from 0.193 in Salemba (2018 to 2020) to 0.097 in Cinere (2020 to May 2024). Many of the new ties in Cinere stood alone, with little connection to either old or new organizations. Weak ties between partners make it harder to pass on what a program has learned.

The information sharing tab holds the detail that explains PKBM Hasanah's complaint. At the local level, the partner often owns the program's technical framework, not LPKA. PKBM Hasanah designs the education and training curriculum, so it decides whether that curriculum is passed on. **When the framework sits with the partner, the program depends on that partner staying.**

## A model for programs that last

We drew the findings into a model with four aspects that keep coaching programs sustainable, and turned each into a step LPKA can take.

<div class="table-wrap"><table><thead><tr><th>Aspect</th><th>Step for LPKA</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Oriented to children's needs</td><td>Survey the children's needs across infrastructure, education, health, religion, personality, and social support, then direct resources there</td></tr><tr><td>A clear technical framework</td><td>Write each program with an SOP, measurable goals, a timeline, resources, and monitoring, so it survives a change of people or partner</td></tr><tr><td>Actors at every level</td><td>Map relevant actors by level and affiliation before involving them in a program</td></tr><tr><td>Continuous evaluation</td><td>Build a standard evaluation scheme with clear indicators that a new partner can take over</td></tr></tbody></table></div>

We also made three recommendations above LPKA. The regional office of the Ministry of Law and Human Rights should identify programs that fit each LPKA's own characteristics. Ditjenpas guidelines should give regional offices more flexible operational authority to work with local partners. Each coaching program should involve the collaborators relevant to it.

The study used website articles, which capture only what LPKA chose to publish, and it treats each period as a static snapshot. My thesis a year later rebuilt the network from 1,789 Instagram posts and found the same direction of change with a larger dataset. That analysis is in [the thesis explainer](article.html?slug=lpka-network-governance).
