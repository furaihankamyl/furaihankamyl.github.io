Undergraduate thesis · 2025 · Social network analysis

When a juvenile institution moves, who runs its network?

No dataset of LPKA Jakarta's partners existed, so I built one from 1,789 Instagram posts and checked it against 11 interviews. The move to Cinere put LPKA at the center of its network, and the network grew faster than its capacity to lead it.

What a notary costs a new company.

Indonesia still requires a notary to register a PT. Comparing 190 countries showed what that requirement adds, and two reforms abroad showed how to remove it.
